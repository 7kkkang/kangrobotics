About

I am a researcher working on robotics and artificial intelligence, with a focus on dual-arm robot motion planning, trajectory learning, and reliable manipulation in structured yet complex task environments.

My research is motivated by intelligent biochemical laboratory automation, where robots need to perform precise, safe, and robust operations involving bottles, tubes, racks, pipettes, and other experimental tools. I am particularly interested in how robots can learn reusable motion patterns from demonstrations and generalize them to new task configurations while maintaining stability, safety, and task reliability.

Research Interests

My research focuses on robot skill learning and reliable manipulation in structured but variable environments. I am especially interested in the gap between demonstrated motions and generalized robot execution, and in developing motion planning and control methods that improve the stability, safety, and adaptability of robot operations.
